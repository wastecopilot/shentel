FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions 

Installation, speeds and switching — everything you need to feel confident before you order.

Call to order

Professional installation, with WiFi coverage set up for your home.

  • Once your order is placed, you choose an installation date and time that suits your schedule. A professional technician sets up your service and equipment, and with Wall-to-Wall WiFi they’ll certify coverage throughout your home before they leave.

  • Shentel Internet plans range from 200 Mbps up to 1 Gig, depending on your location. On the 1 Gig plan, download speeds typically reach up to 940 Mbps because some capacity is reserved to deliver the data, and your in-home equipment should be Gig-E capable to enjoy the full benefit.

  • 200 Mbps suits light streaming and browsing. 400 Mbps handles online gaming, working from home and multiple TV streams. 800 Mbps is built for several people working from home and simultaneous 4K streams. 1 Gig is ideal for power users, heavy gaming and smart homes with many connected devices.

  • Shentel Internet has no annual contract. Promotional rates last 12 to 36 months depending on the speed, and the 800 Mbps and 1 Gig plans include a 2-year and 3-year price guarantee respectively.

  • Order your new Shentel service and schedule installation first. Once you’re up and running, cancel your previous provider so you never lose your connection during the changeover.

  • Each Internet plan includes a data allowance that varies by product. If your household streams and downloads heavily, an unlimited data option is available for an additional charge.

  • Yes. Shentel TV streams over Shentel postpaid Internet. You can watch with the optional Streaming TV device ($5.95/mo) or on supported Android, iOS and Smart TV apps with no rental fee.

  • Shentel services come with a 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee, so you can try your new service with confidence.
